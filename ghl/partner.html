— Investor FAQ —

Good questions, clear answers.

What is Terrasis?

Terrasis is a new four-hectare resort destination being developed by the team behind Don Henrico. It's designed as a premium events and leisure destination — multiple event pavilions, overnight villas, dining, and recreational amenities.

What's the minimum investment?

The minimum entry is ₱500,000, with a target raise of ₱75M for Phase 1 development. Investment tiers and terms are detailed in the investor brief.

What stage is the project at?

Early stage — land identified, concept designed, permits underway. Phase 1 (land preparation and groundbreaking) is targeted for Q3 2026.

How are returns generated?

From the resort's revenue once operating — events, overnight stays, dining, and rentals. Projected ROI and distribution model are in the brief.
