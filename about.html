— Our Heritage —

Where Every Story Begins

Founded in 1993 in the heart of Baguio City, Don Henrico began as a dream to create a place where families could gather and celebrate life's most meaningful moments. What started as a passion for hospitality has grown into a sanctuary for those seeking a private, premium escape.

Our resort was designed with one philosophy in mind: to provide the comfort and warmth needed for an unforgettable escape. From the first welcome to the final goodbye, we ensure your time here feels easy and truly special.
